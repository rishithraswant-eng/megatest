WHAT CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Hardware baseline
   Before: exact QIDK chipset/runtime was an open item.
   Now: use M1's Snapdragon 8 Elite SM8750P and QAIRT/SNPE 2.47 as the
   declared target; still record actual RAM, OS, runtime and model backends.
   The regular-phone scaffold is a development target, not kit evidence.

2. Model responsibilities
   Before: CfC or LFM alternatives; LNN classification, dialogue acts and
   extraction; no separate Laya stage.
   Now: INT8 CfC only filters relevant sentences; LLM extracts grounded
   fields and chooses local calls; selected INT8 Laya supplies incident
   classification in one non-autoregressive pass. ASR/LLM/TTS shortlists
   remain benchmark choices. Relevant replies, including unknown answers,
   negations and corrections, must survive filtering.

3. Language and concurrency
   Before: Hindi-handling owner undecided; generic pipeline wiring.
   Now: speech path converts English/Hindi/Hinglish into English before
   CfC/LLM, with original evidence retained. Benchmark a separate local
   conversion step if the ASR export needs one. CfC screens incoming
   sentences while the LLM processes previously accepted context.
   English follow-up comprehension by Hindi speakers is an open test.

4. Classification and deterministic policy
   Now explicit: 28 types + undetermined/none, probability output, and
   < 0.6 top-2 confirmation proposals. Two incidents need explicit
   grouping proposals; one top-1 label is insufficient. The local taxonomy
   actually contains 28 types and 6 departments.
   App code still owns department/form routing, P1-P4/SLA/role rules,
   safety-first questioning, unknown-field states and draft creation.
   The old TRD's Low/Medium/High classify_priority API conflicts with M1's
   deterministic P1-P4 policy: reconcile it explicitly at the adapter.

5. Evidence and acceptance gates
   Added: dataset/artifact inventory, conversation-level split and leakage
   audits, pre/post-INT8 metrics, and independent human EN/HI/Hinglish audio.
   M1's 1,164 train + 128 test conversations are declared dataset counts,
   not newly verified files. M1's CfC 0.7-0.9 ms / 5.1-5.5 MB are reported
   results to reproduce, not measurements made during this plan edit.
   Preserve P95 <100 ms listening, <150 ms interruption, <1 s cached
   question, <2 s LLM question; log clock boundaries and combined load.

6. Android and real-engine handoff
   Added: nonblocking PCM bridge, sample-rate conversion, pause-based
   turn endings, transcript revisions, bounded queues, echo cancellation,
   stale session/turn/draft result rejection and offline artifact manifests.
   Current scaffold is capture-only: manual WAV recording and its two-minute
   safety cap do not implement M1's conversational turn policy.
   Natural conversation capture belongs to Phase 2 implementation.

IMPACT ON EXISTING PHASE 3-4 WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Phase 3-5 scope text in the plan was retained. The team's reported
webapp implementation/tests remain the starting point; their source and
fixtures were not supplied or inspected in this edit. Android readiness
and compatibility are therefore pending checks, not established facts.

Keep existing process_turn output keys and enum meanings as the baseline.
Add versioned metadata or an explicit adapter for relevance, Laya outcomes,
grouping, streaming and job identity. Optional metadata must remain safe
for consumers that do not understand it; test exact parser behavior.
Do not silently replace confidence with a different shape, introduce fake
incident IDs, rename provenance values or migrate priority labels.
The original 03_TRD model-role/priority statements remain in that file;
the M1-aligned Phase 1-2 notes take precedence for this work.
